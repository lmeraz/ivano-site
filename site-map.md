# Site Map

This shows what pages your site will have and where each piece of your content goes. Use this as a reference while filling out the survey and gathering your photos — it helps you understand *why* we're asking for each piece.

The survey section and asset category are noted in parentheses so you can see the connection.

---

## Home Page

The first thing visitors see. It should immediately communicate who you are, what you do, and why someone should attend your events.

```
Home Page
│
├── Hero Section
│   Your name, professional title, and a striking full-width image.
│   (Survey: Section 1 — About You)
│   (Photos: Portraits or Performance)
│
├── Core Pillars
│   3-5 short statements about what makes your work unique.
│   (Survey: Section 3 — Philosophy)
│
├── Featured Events
│   1-3 upcoming events highlighted with dates, location, and a link to register.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│   (Photos: Teaching or Event Atmosphere)
│
├── Testimonial Highlight
│   One standout quote from a past participant.
│   (Survey: Section 4 — Social Proof)
│
└── Call to Action
    A button linking to the events page or a registration link.
    (Survey: Section 8 — Site Functionality)
```

---

## About Page

The page where visitors learn your story. This is where your personality, philosophy, and credibility come through.

```
About Page
│
├── Bio
│   Your background story — training, experience, what shaped you.
│   (Survey: Section 1 — About You)
│   (Photos: Portraits)
│
├── Teaching / Facilitation Philosophy
│   What guides your approach and what makes it different.
│   (Survey: Section 3 — Philosophy)
│
├── Ideal Participant
│   Who your events are for — helps visitors self-select.
│   (Survey: Section 3 — Philosophy)
│
├── Photo Gallery
│   A selection of images showing you in action and the world of your events.
│   (Photos: Teaching, Performance, Group, Atmosphere)
│
└── Quotes
    Phrases or mantras that capture your philosophy.
    (Survey: Section 3 — Philosophy)
```

---

## Events Page

The main hub for all your events — upcoming, waitlisted, and past.

```
Events Page
│
├── Upcoming Events (Registration Open)
│   Full details for events currently accepting registrations.
│   Each event card shows: name, dates, location, price, and a register button.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│   (Photos: Teaching or Event Atmosphere)
│
├── Waitlist Events
│   Events that are full but accepting waitlist signups.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── Coming Soon
│   Events announced but not yet open for registration.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── Past Events
│   A list of events you've already held — establishes your track record.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Past Events)
│
├── Testimonials
│   Participant feedback displayed alongside event listings.
│   (Survey: Section 4 — Social Proof)
│
└── FAQs
    Common questions and answers about attending your events.
    (Survey: Section 4 — Social Proof)
```

---

## Individual Event Page (one per event)

Each event gets its own detailed page with everything a visitor needs to decide and register.

```
Event Detail Page
│
├── Event Title & Badge
│   Name and a short label (e.g., "Los Angeles Intensive").
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── Description
│   Full description, lead paragraph, and module overview.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── Who It's For
│   A list of who this event is designed for.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── What You Leave With
│   Learning outcomes and takeaways.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── Session Schedule
│   A breakdown of the typical session flow.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── Logistics
│   Dates, venue, format, level, capacity, price.
│   (Survey: Section 2 — Your Events)
│
├── Registration
│   A button linking to registration (external link or email).
│   (Survey: Section 8 — Site Functionality)
│
└── Event Image
    A photo representing this specific event.
    (Photos: Teaching or Event Atmosphere)
```

## Contact Page

How visitors reach you — organized by purpose so the right messages go to the right place.

```
Contact Page
│
├── Headline & Description
│   A short welcome message inviting visitors to get in touch.
│   (Survey: Section 5 — Contact & Channels)
│
├── Contact Channels
│   Separate options for different purposes:
│   e.g., Workshop Registration, General Inquiry, Collaboration, Private Sessions
│   Each shows a description and a link (email or form).
│   (Survey: Section 5 — Contact & Channels)
│
└── Social Links
    Instagram, YouTube, or any other profiles.
    (Survey: Section 5 — Contact & Channels)
```

---

## Visual Direction

The overall look and feel of the site is shaped by your answers in Sections 7 and 8 of the survey:

- **Colors and mood** come from Section 7
- **Photography style** comes from Section 7 + the photos you provide
- **Registration flow and features** come from Section 8

---

## Summary: What Goes Where

| What You Provide | Where It Appears |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Your name, title, bio | Home hero, About page |
| Your philosophy and approach | Home pillars, About page |
| Event details | Events page, Individual event pages |
| Past events | Events page (credibility section) |
| Testimonials | Home page, Events page, About page |
| FAQs | Events page |
| Contact info & channels | Contact page, site footer |
| Brand inspiration & mood | Overall site design |
| Portrait photos | About page, Home hero, social sharing |
| Teaching photos | Event pages, Home page, gallery |
| Atmosphere photos | Event pages, gallery, backgrounds |
| Performance photos | Home hero, About page |
| Group photos | About page, Events page |
