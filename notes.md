## CV / Bio

Ivano Conte is a theatre pedagogue and movement teacher trained in the Lecoq pedagogy. A graduate of Il Cantiere Teatrale in Rome, where he later became a founding member and teacher, he has studied with renowned masters including Norman Taylor, Amy Russell, Philippe Gaulier, Lassaad Saidi, Antonio Fava, Jose Piris Pereda, Mai Rojas, Pablo Ibarluzea, Miguel Angel Gutierrez, Vladimir Olshansky, Carlo Boso, and Emmanuel Gallot-Lavallee.

Ivano is an Associate Lecoq Pedagogue with Embodied Poetics, where he contributes to the transmission of Jacques Lecoq's methodology, focusing on movement analysis, Commedia dell'Arte, Bouffons, and the creative process. He has taught internationally, leading workshops in Italy and Canada. In addition, Ivano is a certified Feldenkrais Method practitioner, integrating somatic awareness into actor training to deepen presence, precision, and creative freedom. His approach blends rigorous physical discipline with imaginative exploration, guiding performers toward authenticity, expressivity, and a living theatrical presence.

## Philosophy

### Teaching Approach

My teaching philosophy is based on play within the group and on creating a space that is open to experimentation -- and also to mistakes -- with the understanding that mistakes lead us to learn something and should not be demonized. I believe that a class in which people feel free to try, and even to "be terrible," is a class that is free to discover itself.

I try to be the first to step into this mindset, bringing a teaching style that is warm, smiling, and present, yet firm when necessary so that students can truly learn. I aim to stay close to my students, so they feel supported and open to listening to what is being offered to them.

Perhaps also because of my relatively young age, I do not yet feel the need to build that large wall that often stands between teacher and student, where the teacher is placed on a higher or more distant level. Of course, if someone comes to study with me, it is because they want something from me, and in a certain sense I do hold a position of greater knowledge. At the same time, I believe that breaking down this "fourth wall" between myself and the student helps me better understand what they truly need.

### Ideal Student

Someone who is willing to step up and truly put themselves on the line, who is not afraid to get their hands dirty. This does not exclude people who feel insecure -- on the contrary, I believe they are the best to have in this kind of work. As someone who is insecure, shy, and introverted myself, I found in "masked" play the freedom to express myself and then bring that freedom into real life.

I do not believe that theatre is therapy, but I do believe that physical work leads to changes we can truly feel, allowing us to discover something new about ourselves.
