## Section 3 — Your Teaching / Facilitation Philosophy

**What principles guide how you lead your events?**

> Example: My teaching philosophy is based on play within the group and creating a space open to experimentation — and to mistakes — with the understanding that mistakes lead us to learn. I believe a class where people feel free to try, and even to "be terrible," is a class that is free to discover itself.

**What makes your approach different from others in your field?**

> Example: I combine movement training, mask work, Commedia dell'Arte, mimodynamics, clown, bouffon, and the Feldenkrais Method. I see the performer not as a container to fill, but as an artist with a physical intelligence already present — one that can be sharpened, questioned, and expanded.

**Describe your ideal participant — who thrives in your events?**

> Example: Someone willing to step up and truly put themselves on the line, who is not afraid to get their hands dirty. This does not exclude people who feel insecure — on the contrary, I believe they are the best to have in this kind of work. As someone who is insecure and introverted myself, I found in "masked" play the freedom to express myself.

**Any quotes, mantras, or phrases you come back to?**

> Example: "The body is the foundation of storytelling." "Movement can think, argue, reveal, question, and transform." "The mask is nothing more than a piece of leather — the only truly essential tool on stage is ourselves and our body."
