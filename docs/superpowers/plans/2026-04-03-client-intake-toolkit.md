# Client Intake Toolkit Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Create four reusable markdown documents that capture everything needed from a client to replicate an events/personality-driven site.

**Architecture:** Four standalone `.md` files in the project root — three client-facing (survey, asset checklist, site map) and one developer-facing (developer notes). Each file is self-contained and requires no tooling to use. Example answers are drawn from the Ivano Workshops site raw materials.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown only. No code, no dependencies.

```markdown
# Asset Checklist

This is a list of photos and files we need from you to build your site. Don't stress about getting everything perfect — we'll work with what you have. But the more you can provide, the better your site will look.

---

## Photos

Organize your photos into folders by category and share them via Google Drive, Dropbox, or a similar service.

### Portraits / Headshots
**What these are for:** Your about page, bio section, and the image that appears when your site is shared on social media.

**How many:** 5-10

**Guidance:**
- At least one clean, well-lit headshot
- A few in different settings — indoor, outdoor, casual, professional
- Show your personality — these should feel like *you*, not a corporate photo

---

### Teaching / Facilitating in Action
**What these are for:** Event pages, homepage, and anywhere we show what your events actually look like.

**How many:** 8-15

**Guidance:**
- You actively leading, demonstrating, or interacting with participants
- Different moments — instruction, hands-on work, group exercises
- Candid is great — these don't need to be posed

---

### Event Atmosphere
**What these are for:** Event pages, gallery, and background images throughout the site.

**How many:** 5-10

**Guidance:**
- Wide shots of the space and the energy in the room
- Candid moments — people laughing, focused, collaborating
- The venue itself — inside, outside, signage

---

### Performance / Demos
**What these are for:** Homepage hero image, credibility section, and visual storytelling.

**How many:** 3-5

**Guidance:**
- You doing the thing you teach — on stage, in performance, demonstrating
- High-energy, visually striking moments work best here
- If you don't perform publicly, skip this category

---

### Group / Ensemble
**What these are for:** Building community feel — testimonials section, about page, event pages.

**How many:** 3-5

**Guidance:**
- Participants together — group shots, collaborative work, end-of-workshop photos
- Shows that your events create a community, not just an audience

---

## File Guidelines

**Format:** JPG or PNG. Phone photos are absolutely fine — we handle all the technical optimization.

**Naming:** Use descriptive names so we know what's what:
- Good: `portrait-outdoor-1.jpg`, `workshop-mask-session.jpg`, `group-final-day.jpg`
- Bad: `IMG_4392.HEIC`, `photo (3).jpg`, `Screen Shot 2026-01-15.png`

**Resolution:** Send the highest quality you have. Don't downsize, crop, or filter before sending. We'll handle all of that.

**Delivery:** Share one folder with subfolders per category:

```
Your Name - Site Photos/
├── portraits/
├── teaching/
├── atmosphere/
├── performance/
└── group/
```

---

## Optional Extras

These aren't required, but share them if you have them:

- **Logo** — if you have one (any format: PNG, SVG, PDF, AI)
- **Promotional flyers or PDFs** — past event flyers, program guides, anything you've designed before
- **Video links** — YouTube or Vimeo links if you want video embedded on your site later
- **Press or media features** — articles, interviews, reviews that mention you or your events
```
