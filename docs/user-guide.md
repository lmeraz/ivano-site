# Ivano Workshops — Content Guide

Welcome! This guide explains how to update your website content without needing to touch any code. Everything is managed through a simple admin panel.

---

## Signing In

Go to: **ivanoworkshops.com/keystatic**

You will be asked to sign in with your GitHub account. Once signed in, you will see the content editor dashboard.

---

## The Dashboard

The left sidebar shows everything you can edit:

- **Workshops** — your upcoming public workshops
- **Past Workshops** — the archive of completed workshops
- **Site Settings** — your name, email, Instagram link, site description
- **Home Page** — the stats, pillars, and process steps on the home page
- **About Page** — your bio, teaching quotes, and ideal student descriptions
- **Contact Page** — the contact channels and hero text
- **Testimonials** — student testimonials
- **FAQs** — frequently asked questions

---

## Editing a Workshop

1. Click **Workshops** in the sidebar
2. Click the workshop you want to edit
3. Make your changes (see fields below)
4. Click **Save** in the top-right corner

### Key workshop fields

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The main name of the workshop |
| **Date Label** | What shows on the site, e.g. "May 10–12, 2025" |
| **Start Date / End Date** | The actual dates in ISO format, e.g. `2025-05-10` |
| **Location / Venue** | City and venue name |
| **Status** | Choose: Registration Open, Waitlist, or Coming Soon |
| **Status Label** | Optional override text for the status badge |
| **Price Label** | e.g. "€350 early bird" |
| **Registration URL** | The Eventbrite or booking link |
| **Featured on Home Page** | Tick this to show the workshop on the home page |

### Changing a workshop status

Find the **Status** dropdown on the workshop edit page. Options are:

- **Registration Open** — shows a "Register" button
- **Waitlist** — shows a waitlist message
- **Coming Soon** — shows the workshop is announced but not open yet

---

## Adding a New Workshop

1. Click **Workshops** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create entry** (top-right)
3. Fill in all the required fields (marked with an asterisk)
4. To show it on the home page, tick the **Featured on Home Page** checkbox
5. Click **Save**

The workshop will appear on the site within about a minute.

---

## Removing a Workshop

1. Click **Workshops** in the sidebar
2. Click the workshop you want to remove
3. Click the **Delete** button (usually at the bottom or in a menu)
4. Confirm the deletion

If you just want to hide a workshop without deleting it, change its status to **Coming Soon** and untick **Featured on Home Page**.

---

## Editing Testimonials

1. Click **Testimonials** in the sidebar
2. You will see a list of all testimonials
3. Click one to edit the name, credit, and quote
4. To add a new one, click **Add item** at the bottom of the list
5. To remove one, click the bin icon next to it
6. Click **Save** when done

---

## Editing FAQs

1. Click **FAQs** in the sidebar
2. Edit, add, or remove questions and answers
3. Click **Save** when done

---

## Editing Site Info

Click **Site Settings** to update:

- Your name and role
- Email address
- Instagram link
- Site description (used in Google search results)

Click **Save** when done.

---

## How Long Until Changes Go Live?

After you save, changes go live in **about 1 minute**. Netlify (the hosting service) automatically rebuilds the site whenever content changes.

If the site does not update after a couple of minutes, try a hard refresh in your browser (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows).

---

## What You Cannot Change Here

Some things require a developer to update:

- The visual design, fonts, and colours
- The page layout and structure
- Adding entirely new sections or pages
- Changing how the navigation works

For those changes, contact your developer.

---

## Need Help?

If something is not working or you are unsure about a field, reach out to your developer. Please include a screenshot if you can — it makes it much easier to help quickly.
